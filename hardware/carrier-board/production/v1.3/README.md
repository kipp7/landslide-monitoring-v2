## Electrical Baseline

- Board outline: `170 x 115 mm`, two copper layers, closed rounded rectangle.
- `U5.7` and `U8.7` are assigned to system `GND`.
- `U5.7 -> D1.3` and `U8.7 -> D2.3` use `1.1 mm` top-layer ground tracks.
- `D1/D2` are `SM712.TCT` RS485 TVS devices with their common pins at `GND`.
- `F1/F2` are `MF-MSMF020-2` resettable fuses from `VBAT_SW` to the two sensor
  branches.
- Battery sensing is `VBAT_SW -> R1 100k -> VBAT_DIV -> R4 27k -> GND`, with
  `R5 1k` from `VBAT_DIV` to `VBAT_SENSE`, `C1 100nF` to ground, and
  `D3 BAT54S,215` clamping the ADC node to `3V3_SYS/GND`.
- `J6/J7` are duplicated eight-position `KF2EDGR-2.5-8P` interfaces:
  `V+, GND, A, B, V+, GND, A, B`.
- All standalone TP footprints and `SW2` were removed. The optional DL-XLS1
  load-switch function was not implemented.
- The two GPS retention holes and all other NPTH holes remain `3.2 mm`.

Connecting the RS485 module field-side ground pins to system ground bypasses
galvanic isolation. This is intentional for this prototype because the sensors
and controller share the same battery and system ground. The modules remain in
the BOM for level conversion and RS485 signaling, not for end-to-end isolation.

## Last Reported Order Settings

- FR-4, 2 layers, `170 x 115 mm`, quantity 5.
- `1.6 mm` finished thickness, TG135 material, `1 oz` outer copper.
- White solder mask, black ordinary silkscreen, tented vias.
- Leaded HASL, minimum drill option `0.3 mm`.
- AOI plus flying-probe test; production-proof confirmation requested.

The ordinary top-silkscreen Gerber does not contain the imported logo at its
intended location. A colorful-silkscreen payload is present, but the last
reported order used ordinary silkscreen. The manufacturer production proof is
the authority for whether the logo will be printed.
