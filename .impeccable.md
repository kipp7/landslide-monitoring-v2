## Design Context

### Users

The primary users are Chinese-speaking competition demonstrators, monitoring
operators, and field personnel. They use a phone to scan current landslide
risk, inspect A/B/C monitoring nodes, understand alarms, open map guidance,
and ask Hermes to run bounded diagnostic tasks. The interface must remain
readable outdoors, under time pressure, and with one hand.

### Brand Personality

Calm, vigilant, and evidence-led. The product should feel like a mature field
instrument rather than a generic admin dashboard, a marketing page, or a
science-fiction AI demo. Normal operation stays quiet; risk and failure states
become unmistakable without decorative alarmism.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, high-contrast "field situation terminal" direction tailored to
HarmonyOS. Prefer unframed information bands, lists, dividers, and purposeful
surfaces over repeated white cards. Use a restrained mineral-green primary,
charcoal text, cool tinted neutrals, and semantic amber/red only for actual
attention and danger. Keep native Chinese system typography for complete glyph
coverage, instant rendering, and platform consistency. The Hermes screen may
borrow the clarity and composer ergonomics of mature mobile AI products, but
must expose monitoring evidence, planner source, and task state honestly.

### Design Principles

1. Risk before metrics: active risk, offline nodes, and data age outrank volume
   statistics and decorative summaries.
2. Evidence before claims: distinguish live, cached, stale, unavailable, model
   planned, and deterministic fallback states in plain Chinese.
3. Quiet when normal: reserve strong color and motion for real warnings and
   current map alarms.
4. Field-readable: body text is at least 14 vp, secondary text normally 12-13
   vp, controls are at least 44 vp, and long Chinese labels can wrap safely.
5. Native and efficient: preserve ArkUI interaction patterns, stable layout,
   scroll position, cache behavior, SSE alarms, and existing API contracts.

## Design Brief

### Objective

Raise the HarmonyOS App from a functional prototype to a coherent monitoring
and AI operations product without changing monitoring, alert, map, cache, or
Hermes execution behavior.

### Information Architecture

- Overview: highest risk and action entry first; node availability and data
  freshness second; trends and system volume after operational priorities.
- Stations: distribution map and station selection first, then concise node
  rows with status, latest evidence, and data age.
- AI: conversation first, bounded tools progressively disclosed, visible
  planner source, RK3568 status, and task evidence without dashboard clutter.
- My: operational preferences and diagnostics grouped as settings; destructive
  session/server actions use explicit language.

### Visual System

- 4 vp spacing base with 8/12/16/24/32 vp rhythm.
- Type roles: 12 metadata, 14 body, 17 section, 22 page, 30 primary risk/value.
- Radius roles: 6 vp compact controls, 8 vp actionable surfaces, 18-24 vp only
  for chat bubbles and the composer.
- Avoid nested cards, identical metric grids, decorative shadows, gradients,
  colored side stripes, and motion that does not communicate state.

### Quality Bar

Flagship competition prototype: every core screen must compile for HarmonyOS
5.0.1(13), work on the phone emulator, preserve current behavior, and pass a
visual screenshot review at the existing 1260x2720 phone viewport.
