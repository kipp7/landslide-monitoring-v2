## Design Context

### Users

The HarmonyOS app is used by Chinese-speaking field operators and competition judges who need to monitor a landslide site from a phone. Their primary jobs are to identify current risk, verify station and node freshness, inspect alert evidence and location, and issue bounded Hermes tasks to the RK3568 edge agent. The interface must remain legible outdoors, under time pressure, and during intermittent connectivity.

### Brand Personality

Calm, vigilant, and credible. The product should feel like a mature field instrument rather than a generic administration dashboard or a personal demo. Urgency is reserved for real risk and communication failures.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a light, high-contrast, industrial-utilitarian mobile interface with concise Chinese labels, restrained green system accents, and red/orange only for real risk states. Prefer clear hierarchy, full-width operational sections, and compact controls over decorative cards. Motion should be short and purposeful, helping users understand selection, expansion, message arrival, recovery, and active alerts.

### Design Principles

- Put current risk, offline nodes, data source, and update time before aggregate metrics.
- Never fabricate online state, GPS, conductivity, AI evidence, or real-time values.
- Keep interactive targets at least 44 vp and support one-handed operation on a phone.
- Distinguish loading, empty, cached, offline, stale, success, and failure states explicitly.
- Preserve monitoring context across alert, map, device, and Hermes task flows.
- Avoid gradients, glass effects, nested cards, decorative pulsing, and redundant explanatory copy.
